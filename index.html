SQUISHIA

A Fantasy Action RPG

Explore a mysterious world, battle monsters, discover legendary weapons, and uncover the secrets behind Malice's rule.

Play Now GitHub

Story

100 years after the great hero Samknight saved the world, a new darkness has appeared. Malice has taken control of the kingdom, and Squishy, one of the kingdom's strongest warriors, has been sent on a mission to stop him. With the help of Ockey, a mysterious companion, Squishy must explore the world and uncover the truth.

Features

⚔ Combat

Battle enemies using weapons, abilities, and strategy.

🗺 Exploration

Discover new areas filled with secrets and challenges.

🎒 Inventory

Collect items, coins, and upgrades.

🧙 Ockey

Travel with your companion and improve their abilities.

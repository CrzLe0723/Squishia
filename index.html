THE JOURNEY BEGINS

The Story

One hundred years after the great hero Samknight saved the world, a new threat has risen.

Malice, son of the Dark Lord, has taken control of the land. Squishy, one of the kingdom's strongest warriors, has been sent by the queen on a mission to find him.

Joined by Ockey, Squishy must explore the world, grow stronger, and uncover what stands between them and Malice.
